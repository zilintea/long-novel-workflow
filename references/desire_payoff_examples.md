# Desire-payoff examples

One concrete payoff per desire, per language. These are patterns, not plots —
never copy them verbatim into a book; invent the book's own payoff.

## Curiosity （好奇）

- zh: 第一章结尾，主角发现抽屉里的信是自己笔迹，日期却是三年后。
- en: The letter in the drawer is in her own handwriting. It is dated three years from now.

## Identification-upgrade （代入升级）

- zh: 一直被说"废物"的学徒，当众用师父教过的最基础一招，赢了大师兄。
- en: The apprentice everyone called useless wins with the one basic move his master taught him — in front of the whole hall.

## Revenge-compensation （复仇补偿）

- zh: 当年把主角赶出家门的人，现在要排队求主角在文件上签字。
- en: The people who threw her out now queue to ask her to sign the paper that saves them.

## Being-loved （被爱拉扯）

- zh: 对方明明可以走，却留下来替主角挡了一刀，然后说"别多想，我只是路过"。
- en: He could have walked away. He stayed, took the blow, and said, "Don't read into it. I was just passing."

## Escape-comfort （逃逸安慰）

- zh: 逃了一整章之后，主角终于推开一扇门，里面是热汤、灯光，和一个等她的人。
- en: After running for a whole chapter, she pushes a door open: hot soup, lamplight, and someone waiting for her.
