# Knows Lists — 《回声》

What each character knows, and in which chapter they learned it.
A character may guess, suspect or misread — never *know* what they were
never told.

## 沈昭

- Knows she was reborn (chapter 1 — she lived it).
- Knows the drawer was locked and the key is missing (chapter 1 — she checked).
- Knows the unsigned letter exists (chapter 2 — she found it in the study).
- Does NOT know who wrote the letter (open — F02).
- Does NOT know who the stranger in the photograph is (open — F03).

## 陆明远

- Knows the dock warehouse deal is his plan (chapter 1 — his plan).
- Does NOT know 沈昭 was reborn (hard constraint — he must never learn it
  from the narration, only from evidence in a later chapter).
- Does NOT know about the photograph (chapter 3 — he was not there).

## 顾言舟

- Knows his bookshop paper has a watermark (chapter 2 — it is his paper).
- Does NOT know who used his paper for the letter (open — F02).
- Does NOT know 沈昭 was reborn.
