# TRAFFIC — where readers actually come from

> Numbers below are a **search snapshot dated 2026-11-13**, from public
> reporting (QuestMobile via press coverage, platform comparisons). They are
> direction, not official figures — re-check before publishing or quoting
> them, and never present them as the skill's own measurement.

## Chinese entry (primary, v1.0.0)

**Fanqie (番茄小说) is the primary entry.** ~260M monthly active users and
~120M daily active users in 2026, over 80% of the free-reading market — an
order of magnitude above Qidian's app (~21M MAU in one third-party table;
Qidian's all-platform MAU is reported near 134M, with paying users falling
from ~9.2M to ~8.2M in H1 2026). Qimao (七猫) is the second free platform
(~67–77M MAU). Jinjiang (晋江) is the female-readership home (79.7M
registered users, 2026-09). Qidian remains the paid-quality platform; it is
not the first entry for a traffic-type serial.

**The entry is not the platform alone.** The loop is: short-video clip
(Douyin) → keyword search → Fanqie read. Fanqie's own creator centre
(番茄达人中心) is the official promotion entry; Xiaohongshu supplies
search + discussion long-tail; Hongguo (红果, short drama, ~315M MAU
2026-03) is the same ByteDance ecosystem. Short-drama clips were ~32% of
top novel ad creatives, small animation ~30%, film clips ~24% — a chapter
must be sliceable into a 3-second hook clip.

**The warning that shapes v1.0:** Fanqie was receiving 5,000+ new books a
day in 2026, almost all AI-generated (up ~1302% year on year). Volume is
not a strategy. The skill's differentiator is the opposite: a chapter that
actually pays its foreshadowing off, a hook that actually lands, prose
without AI flavour — the things bulk generation cannot fake at scale.

## English entry (phase 2)

- **Royal Road** — LitRPG / progression fantasy. The only English platform
  with a genuine new-author discovery engine (Rising Stars), no exclusivity
  contract, monetisation via Patreon. ~121,000 fictions. Genre-narrow by
  design: romance and literary fiction get thin response there.
- **Wattpad** — largest user base, romance / YA / fan fiction, teens to
  twenties, mostly female. Paid Stories is invite-only; the algorithm
  favours already-popular stories. Exposure first, revenue second.
- **Webnovel** — largest catalogue, Qidian's international arm. Contract
  heavy: read exclusivity clauses before signing anything.
- **BookTok** — the English discovery layer: community recommendation,
  romantasy-led. A book needs a talkable hook and a quotable line, not just
  a platform listing.

## What the skill does about traffic

- The market-scan skill (Step 0) checks the target platform *now* — genre,
  hook and title direction are evidence, not taste.
- The packaging skill writes title, cover brief, blurb and keywords for
  that platform's search and recommendation.
- Chapter length and hook cadence are platform-shaped (see `docs/BILINGUAL.md`
  and `data/genres/`), but the continuity kernel never changes for traffic.
